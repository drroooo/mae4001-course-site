    Week 9: Solution Ideation and Medium Selection¶

    10/27 and 10/29

    Learning Objectives¶
    • Generate multiple technological solutions to the identified user problem.
    • Use user evidence and psychological mechanisms to guide solution ideation.
    • Select and justify an appropriate solution concept and technological medium.
    Class Content¶

    Day 1: Divergent Thinking: Silent Solution Ideation¶

    • User evidence and selected psychological mechanisms as ideation inputs
    • In-Class Activity: Design Concept Studio

    Day 2: Convergent Thinking: Concept and Medium Selection¶

    • Assessment and justification of the medium and its affordances
    • In-Class Activity: Design Concept Studio
    Assignments¶
    • Preserve the individual ideation and group concept-selection artifacts.
    • Begin Deliverable 2: Theory-Based Design Proposal.
