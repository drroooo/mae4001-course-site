Technology use is intentionally designed

Day 1

Interface features can direct attention, reduce friction, encourage repetition, and shape what feels socially expected.

  • Design creates possibilities and constraints.
  • Design embodies assumptions about users.
  • Design can produce intended and unintended effects.

The semester project

Learning by doing

Your team will investigate a meaningful user problem and develop a theory-informed technological response.

  • Gather and synthesize user evidence.
  • Use psychological theory to guide design decisions.
  • Prototype, evaluate, and revise one critical user pathway.
